# Payment Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional billing policy for FinAssist AI. All figures are
> synthetic example values.

## Fee Structure

FinAssist AI charges a flat consultation fee per service engagement, billed monthly
for the duration of an active engagement:

- Retirement Planning — $150/month (example rate)
- Investment Advisory Consultation — $175/month (example rate)
- Tax Planning Consultation — $125/month (example rate)
- Estate Planning Guidance — $200/month (example rate)
- Business Financial Consulting — $250/month (example rate)

## Billing Cadence

Invoices are issued on the first business day of each month for the prior month's
active engagement. Payment is due within 15 calendar days of the invoice date.

## Accepted Payment Methods

FinAssist AI accepts payment via bank transfer or major credit card through its
billing portal (fictional, for demonstration purposes only).

## Late Payments

If payment is not received within 30 days of the invoice date, the client's account
status is flagged for advisor follow-up. Services are not automatically suspended;
a human advisor reviews the account before any action is taken.
