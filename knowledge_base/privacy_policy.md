# Privacy Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional privacy policy for FinAssist AI. This document
> describes a fictional data-handling process; no real client data is collected,
> stored, or processed by this project.

## Data Collected

In the fictional FinAssist AI system, client and lead records may include: name,
contact information, service interest, onboarding status, and uploaded documents
(e.g., ID, proof of address, financial statements).

## How Data Is Used

Data is used only to deliver advisory services, verify onboarding requirements, and
maintain accurate client records. It is not sold or shared with third parties for
marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

Active client records are retained for the duration of the engagement. Closed
account records are retained for a fictional 7-year period to reflect a realistic
recordkeeping practice, then eligible for deletion.

## Security

FinAssist AI represents that access to client documents and records is restricted to
authorized advisors and operations staff. In this portfolio project, all data is
synthetic and stored locally for demonstration only — no real personal information
is ever processed.

## Client Rights

Fictional clients may request a copy of their stored information or request account
closure at any time (see `account_closure.md`).
