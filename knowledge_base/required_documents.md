# Required Documents for Onboarding

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional document checklist for FinAssist AI client
> onboarding.

To complete onboarding, every new client must submit the following four documents:

1. **Government-issued ID** — A valid passport, driver's license, or national ID card
   confirming the client's identity.
2. **Proof of Address** — A recent utility bill, lease agreement, or bank statement
   (dated within the last 3 months) confirming the client's current address.
3. **Recent Financial Statement** — A bank or investment account statement from the
   last 60 days, used to understand the client's current financial picture.
4. **Completed Application Form** — The signed FinAssist AI client application form,
   including selected service and contact preferences.

## Verification Rules

- A document is considered "received" only once operations staff (or the FinAssist AI
  assistant, pending human confirmation) has matched it to a client record.
- Documents with illegible, expired, or missing information are treated as **not**
  satisfying the requirement and must be re-requested.
- A client cannot move to `onboarding_status: Complete` until all four documents
  above are marked as received and valid.

## Exceptions

Business Financial Consulting clients may additionally be asked for a business
registration document; this is handled as a case-by-case addition by the assigned
advisor and is not part of the standard four-document checklist.
