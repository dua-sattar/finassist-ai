# Services

> Portfolio demonstration — synthetic service catalog for the fictional company FinAssist AI.

FinAssist AI offers five core advisory services. Clients and leads are associated with
exactly one primary service at a time.

## 1. Retirement Planning
Guidance on building and reviewing a retirement savings strategy, including
contribution planning and long-term goal setting. This is a consultative service and
does not constitute a guarantee of future financial outcomes.

## 2. Investment Advisory Consultation
General consultation sessions to help clients understand investment concepts and
organize their existing portfolios. FinAssist AI advisors do not execute trades or
manage assets directly.

## 3. Tax Planning Consultation
Support in organizing financial records and understanding general tax planning
concepts ahead of filing season. FinAssist AI does not prepare or file tax returns.

## 4. Estate Planning Guidance
Consultation to help clients think through wealth transfer and estate organization
topics, with a recommendation to work alongside a licensed estate attorney for legal
documentation.

## 5. Business Financial Consulting
Advisory support for small business owners on cash-flow organization, budgeting
structure, and financial recordkeeping practices.

## Notice

All FinAssist AI services are advisory and educational in nature. Recommendations
produced by the FinAssist AI assistant are drafts for advisor review and do not
constitute financial, tax, or legal advice.
