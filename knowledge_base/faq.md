# Frequently Asked Questions

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional FAQ for FinAssist AI.

**Q: What documents do I need to complete onboarding?**
A: A government-issued ID, proof of address, a recent financial statement, and a
completed application form. See `required_documents.md` for details.

**Q: How long does onboarding take?**
A: Typically within 10 business days of submitting a signed application, assuming
all required documents are provided promptly. See `client_onboarding.md`.

**Q: How much do FinAssist AI services cost?**
A: Fees vary by service and are billed monthly. See `payment_policy.md` for example
rates.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds may be available within 14 days of billing if fewer than two sessions
were completed in that period. See `refund_policy.md`.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Notify your assigned advisor in writing. There is no closure fee. See
`account_closure.md`.

**Q: How quickly will someone respond to my message?**
A: General inquiries within 1 business day; document-related requests within 2
business days; urgent matters the same business day. See `client_communication.md`.

**Q: Can the AI assistant give me investment or tax advice?**
A: No. The assistant can answer questions about FinAssist AI policies and help with
administrative tasks, but investment, tax, and legal questions are routed to a
licensed human advisor. See `escalation_policy.md`.

**Q: Is my information kept private?**
A: Yes — in this fictional policy, data is used only to deliver services and is not
sold to third parties. See `privacy_policy.md`. Note this entire project uses only
synthetic, non-real data.

**Q: What services does FinAssist AI offer?**
A: Retirement Planning, Investment Advisory Consultation, Tax Planning Consultation,
Estate Planning Guidance, and Business Financial Consulting. See `services.md`.
