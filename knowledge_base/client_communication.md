# Client Communication Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional communication standards for FinAssist AI.

## Response Time Targets

- General inquiries: response within 1 business day.
- Document-related requests (e.g., missing document follow-ups): response within
  2 business days of the document being flagged as outstanding.
- Urgent or escalated matters: same business day (see `escalation_policy.md`).

## Preferred Channels

Email is the primary channel for formal communication (document requests,
onboarding updates, billing notices). The client portal chat is available for quick
questions and is monitored by the FinAssist AI assistant during business hours, with
human advisors reviewing all AI-drafted responses before they are sent.

## Tone and Content Standards

All client-facing communication, whether drafted by a human or by the FinAssist AI
assistant, must:

- Be professional, clear, and free of unexplained jargon.
- Avoid making guarantees about financial outcomes.
- Clearly state next steps and any documents or actions needed from the client.
- Be reviewed and approved by a human advisor before sending, when AI-generated.
