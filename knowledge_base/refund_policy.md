# Refund Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional refund policy for FinAssist AI.

## Eligibility

Clients may request a refund of their most recent monthly consultation fee within
14 calendar days of being billed, provided fewer than two advisory sessions were
conducted during that billing period.

## Non-Refundable Situations

- Fees for months in which two or more advisory sessions were completed.
- Requests submitted more than 14 days after the invoice date.
- One-time onboarding or document-review fees, which FinAssist AI does not charge
  (onboarding is included at no additional cost).

## Process

1. Client submits a refund request to their assigned advisor or through the client
   portal.
2. Operations staff verify session history against the billing period in question.
3. Eligible refunds are processed within 5 business days.
4. All refund decisions require human advisor approval; the FinAssist AI assistant
   may draft a recommendation but cannot approve or issue a refund autonomously.
