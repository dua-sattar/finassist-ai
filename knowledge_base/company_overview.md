# Company Overview

> Portfolio demonstration — FinAssist AI is a fictional company. All information in this document is synthetic and created for demonstration purposes only.

## Who We Are

FinAssist AI is a fictional financial advisory and client services firm founded to help
individuals and small businesses organize their financial lives with clear, personalized
guidance. FinAssist AI is not a bank, broker-dealer, or registered investment advisor;
it exists solely as a demonstration company for this software portfolio project.

## Mission

FinAssist AI's mission is to combine experienced human advisors with modern technology
so that clients receive timely, well-organized support throughout their financial
planning journey — from initial inquiry through ongoing account management.

## What We Do

FinAssist AI offers advisory and consultation services across five core areas:
Retirement Planning, Investment Advisory Consultation, Tax Planning Consultation,
Estate Planning Guidance, and Business Financial Consulting. See `services.md` for
details on each offering.

## How We Work

Every client is paired with a dedicated advisor who oversees onboarding, ongoing
communication, and account management. Internal operations staff support advisors by
reviewing submitted documents, tracking follow-ups, and maintaining accurate client
records in the firm's CRM system.

## Offices

FinAssist AI is a fictional remote-first firm with a single synthetic headquarters
location: 100 Ledger Avenue, Suite 400, Springfield, ST 00000.
