# Account Closure Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional account closure policy for FinAssist AI.

## Client-Initiated Closure

Clients may close their FinAssist AI account at any time by notifying their assigned
advisor in writing (email or client portal message). No penalty or closure fee
applies.

## Notice Period

FinAssist AI requests, but does not require, 10 business days' notice so the advisor
can wrap up any active consultation work and provide a closing summary.

## What Happens on Closure

1. The client's `account_status` is updated to Closed in the CRM.
2. Any open follow-up tasks tied to the account are reviewed and either completed or
   cancelled by the assigned advisor.
3. Billing stops at the end of the current billing cycle; no further invoices are
   issued.
4. Client records are retained per the data retention terms described in
   `privacy_policy.md`.

## Reopening an Account

A closed account can be reopened by contacting FinAssist AI and completing an
abbreviated onboarding review to confirm prior information is still accurate.
