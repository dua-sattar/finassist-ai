# Escalation Policy

> Portfolio demonstration — fictional escalation policy for FinAssist AI.

## When to Escalate

A matter should be escalated from the FinAssist AI assistant or junior staff to a
senior human advisor or manager when it involves:

- A client complaint about service quality or billing.
- A request that falls outside the standard service catalog (see `services.md`).
- Any question involving actual investment recommendations, tax filing, or legal
  advice — the assistant must not answer these directly and must route them to a
  licensed human advisor.
- Repeated missing documents after two follow-up attempts.
- Any suspected data privacy or security concern.

## Escalation Process

1. The issue is logged as a high-priority follow-up task in the CRM.
2. The assigned advisor is notified and given full context (client history, prior
   communications, relevant documents).
3. A manager is looped in for complaints or anything involving potential
   compliance concerns.
4. The FinAssist AI assistant may summarize the situation and suggest next steps,
   but a human must make and carry out the final decision.
